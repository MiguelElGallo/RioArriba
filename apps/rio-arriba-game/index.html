Icons & Points

Drag anywhere on the river to steer. Hold GUN with your other thumb. Drag up/down or hold FAST/SLOW to change speed.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD to fly, Space for the machine gun, X for missiles, P/Esc to pause, Enter to start or retry.

The machine gun fires fast rounds: drones and jets take 2 hits; barges and bridges take 3. Hit flashes, amber damage tint, and missing health marks show damage.

Missiles travel more slowly and destroy a target in one hit. Tap MISSILE when READY, or hold to launch every 1.5 seconds. The countdown shows reloading.

Release both weapons and slow down over a charger to refill. Chargers break in one hit. Destroy bridges to reach the next level.
